The video beta is available to signed-in, non-anonymous accounts when the service is enabled.
Open **Video** in the editor or **Video beta** in the account navigation. Generation requires
qualifying paid credits and an available model configuration. No invitation is required.
There is no guest video trial.
Your videos and results remain private to your account.

## Hotel Lobby template

[Hotel Lobby AI](/video-effects/hotel-lobby-ai) is a separate template for signed-in accounts.
Upload a left and a right photo; no model choice or prompt is required. The configured total
appears before upload. **Available for this video** shows eligible credits, while **Account total**
also includes credits that cannot fund this video. A shortfall message shows how many more
eligible credits are needed. Free credits and payments that do not meet video funding requirements
do not qualify.

Wait for both photos to finish uploading, then choose **Review total credits** and confirm the
final quote. One charge covers the scene and video. Reviewing a quote does not start generation
or reserve credits. If a confirmation is interrupted, checking the same request remains available
even when its reservation has reduced your displayed balance.

## Create a video

Open [Video](/video), choose an available model and **Text to video** or **Image to video**, and
describe the scene or movement. Prompt limits, durations, resolutions, frame shapes, and audio
options follow the selected model's supported Kie parameters. Image-only models offer only
**Image to video**. Models that
follow the reference image's framing do not offer a separate crop.
Prompt checks must complete before generation can start.

Image mode accepts one JPEG, PNG, or WebP within the displayed account limit. A local preview is
not proof of upload or approval. Wait for the server to verify and secure the image. Content review
starts after generation is confirmed.

Visual review checks sampled frames for NSFW and reported special-care signals. It does not
inspect every frame or certify every kind of visual content. The saved output must also meet
the review service's 30-second and 100 MB limits. Incomplete or uncertain review keeps the result
unavailable; task completion alone does not mean content passed. Accepted tasks keep the output
visual-review policy recorded when they were confirmed, even if the service's default changes later.

The initial selection is Kling 2.6, five seconds, with sound disabled. The model selector groups
MiniMax, Seedance, Gemini, Kling, and Veo. The available settings follow each implemented model's
supported Kie parameters. Unsupported provider options and
settings without a configured price or required checks remain unavailable.
Models with a native-audio option can include audio; some models do not offer a sound switch.
There is no video editing, audio upload, or post-generation resolution upgrade.

Choose **Review credit cost** to get a quote. A quote does not reserve credits or start generation.
Read the quoted credits, then confirm within ten minutes. Pricing is shown only when a valid
service price is configured. There is no fixed cutoff date for creating ordinary videos.
Confirmation reserves space for the output along with credits. Insufficient account storage prevents
generation from starting. An unresolved provider request keeps its reservation while the original
attempt is checked.
Video can use only credits backed by a qualifying payment. Free credits, credits with an unknown
payment source, and payment funding that does not cover the required video spend are not eligible.
Changing the model, prompt, image, duration, resolution, shape, or audio invalidates the previous quote.
If the price changes before confirmation, review a new quote and confirm the updated total.
An expired quote also requires a new quote. Neither case automatically submits a replacement
request. An interrupted response still uses **Check the same request**, as described below.

## Progress and interrupted connections

Accepted tasks continue on the server even if you close the page. Open [Video history](/video/history)
with the same account to restore their current status. Only your own tasks appear there.

The workspace distinguishes input review, provider submission, generation, private storage,
output review, and final delivery. It does not estimate a generation percentage. Upload percentages
refer only to bytes sent from your browser.

Output visual confirmation waits for a verified service notification and can briefly retry
temporary connection failures. If the notification does not arrive or the result still
cannot be confirmed, the task is held for review: the video stays unavailable
and credits remain reserved until an operator resolves it. This does not automatically
purchase a replacement generation.

If a confirmation response is interrupted, use **Check the same request**. This keeps the original
request identity and every selected model setting. Do not start a new request just because the first response was lost. If the
provider's acceptance is uncertain, the server checks the original attempt and keeps its credit
reservation; it does not automatically submit another paid generation.

## Credits and private results

The task displays whether its credits are reserved, charged, or released. A task awaiting an
operational review can retain its reservation until its outcome is confirmed. Rejected and failed
tasks show the actual credit status; they do not expose a button that silently buys another generation.

Playback and MP4 download become available only after the saved private video passes required
checks. If a playback link expires, refresh its authorization. Downloads request fresh access.
Other accounts cannot use your task or access your stored output through these pages.
Delivered videos are retained until their recorded expiry unless you delete them first.

If storage needs recovery, the server works with the existing generated result. It does not request
another video from the model. When new video requests are disabled, your existing task status and
eligible results remain available.
