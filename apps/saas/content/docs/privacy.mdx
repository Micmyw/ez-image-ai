EzPic keeps source images and generated results private to their account or temporary guest session.
It does not automatically publish your edits to a gallery. The service still sends the information
needed to hosting, storage, moderation, and image-processing services to complete your request.

Before generation, your text instruction is screened by the enabled content-safety services.
Text checks can use Waffo and Sightengine; they receive the instruction and scan settings, without
your source image, account email, or payment details. Image checks can use SeeAPI and Sightengine;
they receive a short-lived access URL for the private source or generated image. A request awaiting
review or an unavailable safety check cannot proceed to generation or release an unapproved image.
The billing account also records use of its one-time output-block credit waiver; deleting media
or job history does not reset it.

## Access control

Preview and download requests require ownership checks and return short-lived access links. Treat a
signed link as private: someone who receives it may be able to use it until it expires. Download a
copy and choose your own sharing channel when you intend to publish a result.

## How long media is kept

- Public drafts expire within one hour.
- Guest-trial media expires within 24 hours of the job being created. Guest previews are watermarked.
- Registered input and output media currently targets 30 days of retention, with 7 days for failed-job cleanup.

Guest results keep their original expiry when linked to a new account. Deleting an eligible asset
prevents new access links and schedules file cleanup. Billing, security, and backup records may have
different retention periods.

## What you should upload

Upload only material you are authorized to use and avoid including information that is unnecessary
for the requested edit. Content can be rejected by the product's moderation and admission controls.

Read the [private editing guide](/blog/private-image-editing-workflow) for a practical walkthrough,
the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full data and analytics policy, or [contact support](/contact)
for an access or deletion request.

## Report unsafe or prohibited content

Use [Report content](/contact#report-content) to contact the EzPic operator about prohibited
generated images, impersonation, or rights violations. You can report without an account.
Provide a description and any available job, asset, or public page reference. Do not send private
signed links, credentials, or copies of suspected illegal content.

The [Acceptable Use Policy in the Terms of Service](/terms) lists prohibited content,
moderation and enforcement, all four response-time levels, and how to appeal a decision.
Critical child-safety, terrorism, or imminent-harm reports receive an initial response within
2 hours and action within 24 hours, including weekends. Put `URGENT content report` in the
email subject for these cases.
