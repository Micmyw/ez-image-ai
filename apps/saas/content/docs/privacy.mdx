---
title: Privacy and private media
description: Understand how EzPic limits access to uploaded sources, generated results, and account-owned media.
---

EzPic treats uploaded source images and generated results as private media assets. Business state is
stored in EzPic's database; an AI Provider, queue, browser, or storage service does not become the
source of truth for job ownership.

## Access control

Media access is scoped to the owning user or organization. Preview and download requests return
short-lived access URLs only after the server verifies ownership. Provider credentials, model routing,
raw Provider payloads, and storage credentials are not exposed to the browser.

## What you should upload

Upload only material you are authorized to use and avoid including information that is unnecessary
for the requested edit. Content can be rejected by the product's moderation and admission controls.

For the public policy governing the service, read the [EzPic Privacy Policy](/privacy).
