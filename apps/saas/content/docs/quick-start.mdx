## Stay in your workspace

The [homepage](/) has top navigation and a centered editor, with inspiration examples and guides
below. After signing in, it shows your account editor and a **Create image** link in the header.
The homepage has no tool sidebar, whether you are signed in or visiting.

Use **AI Image Tools** or **AI Models** in the header to open the [creation page](/create).
Visitors and signed-in users both get the tool sidebar. Selecting an available model opens the editor
with that model selected; changing models within the workspace keeps your image and prompt. The URL
retains your model choice for refresh and browser navigation.

After signing in, the sidebar also includes **History** and **Assets**. Select your avatar in the
homepage header or the creation sidebar to open account settings. Visitors do not see account settings.
These panels leave your editor and current job in place. Choosing an inspiration example replaces
only the prompt; selecting a source from Assets keeps the prompt and output choices.

When you scroll below the editor, a compact bar lets you return to your prompt or current result.
If browser storage is available, your account draft is retained in this tab for up to one hour across
navigation. Restoring a draft checks the source again and requires a fresh credit review. It does
not save the image file locally or submit another job.

## 1. Choose a source image

Open the [EzPic image editor](/#image-editor) and choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image you are allowed to
edit. Check the file-size limit beside the upload control; the public editor currently accepts up to
10 MB. Use a source where the subject and important details are clearly visible.

Choosing an image prepares a preview in your browser. Continuing starts the upload. If the page
reports that editing is unavailable, you can prepare your instruction, read the guides, or check
availability again. Signing in does not by itself restore an unavailable service.

## 2. Describe the change

Write a concrete instruction for the edit. Describe the change you want to see rather than a general
topic. You can revise the prompt before submitting the job. For example: "Replace the background
with a pale gray studio backdrop. Keep the mug, logo, and viewing angle unchanged."

This is a suggested prompt, not a demonstrated result. For more examples, read
[How to write AI image editing prompts](/blog/ai-image-editing-prompts).

## 3. Check access and output settings

Choose an available image product and output setting. When the guest trial is available, Nano Banana
2 Lite 1K offers one private, watermarked preview without registration. Other options require the
account access shown in the editor. Guest results expire within 24 hours and are not saved to History.

Model menus use matching brand icons across the navigation and editor. The configured catalog includes
twelve models across Nano Banana, GPT Image, and Seedream, including GPT Image 2.5 Flare,
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, and Seedream 4.0. Each menu lists the models currently enabled for use. A **Paid plan** badge marks
models that need a subscription; **Up to 2K** or **Up to 4K** describes a model's maximum resolution.
Choose the actual resolution in output settings. If your account needs a different plan, selecting
the model keeps you in the editor and shows an access notice. Click **View plans** to compare plans,
or **Keep editing** to close the dialog. Your selected model and prompt stay saved even if you have
not uploaded an image yet.

For an account edit, sign in, review the exact credit cost, and confirm the job. Choosing a product or
entering a prompt does not itself purchase a plan. See [Image editing](/docs/image-editing) for output
settings and [Pricing](/pricing) for plan details.

## 4. Review the result

The result panel reports progress and the final outcome. Compare a successful result with its source
and check text, faces, logos, and edges before downloading or publishing. An eligible saved account
result can be used with **Edit again**, which starts a new edit with its own credit charge.

## If an edit does not finish

If the upload fails, check the file format and size, then use the retry action. The page preserves the
selected file and prompt while you remain there. After a job is submitted, use its status and credit
details to check whether it is still processing or has failed. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same
edit while its outcome is uncertain.

[Credits](/docs/credits) explains reservations and releases. [Contact support](/contact) if the status
remains unresolved; include the visible error message, but do not send private access links or passwords.
