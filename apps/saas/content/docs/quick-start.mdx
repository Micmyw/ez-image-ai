## Stay in your workspace

The [homepage](/) has top navigation and a centered editor, with inspiration examples and guides
below. After signing in, it shows your account editor and a **Create image** link in the header.
The homepage has no tool sidebar, whether you are signed in or visiting.

Use **AI Image Tools** to open the [creation page](/create), or browse [AI Models](/models) for a
dedicated page with a preselected generator, creative directions, and an example prompt for each model.
Visitors and signed-in users both get the tool sidebar on the creation page. Changing models within
that workspace keeps your image and prompt. The URL retains the selection for refresh and navigation.

After signing in, the sidebar also includes **History** and **Assets**. Select your avatar in the
homepage header or the creation sidebar to open account settings. Visitors do not see account settings.
These panels leave your editor and current job in place. Choosing an inspiration example replaces
only the prompt; selecting a source from Assets keeps the prompt and output choices.

When you scroll below the editor, a compact bar lets you return to your prompt or current result.
If browser storage is available, your account draft is retained in this tab for up to one hour across
navigation. Restoring a draft checks the source again and refreshes the credit price when submitted. It does
not save the image file locally or submit another job.

## 1. Start with text or an optional reference

To create a new image, leave the reference empty. Describe the subject, composition, light, and style.
For example: "A cobalt ceramic vase beside a large window, warm afternoon light, a cream wall,
editorial product photography, no lettering."

Click **Sign in to generate** to continue. Your prompt, selected model, and output settings stay in
this tab for up to one hour. After signing in, the **Generate image** button shows the credit cost.
Click it once to run the safety check and start generation.
No upload is required for this path. Available models and account credits still apply.

To edit or guide a result with an existing image, choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image you are allowed
to use in the [workspace](/#image-editor). Check the size limit beside the upload control; the guest editor accepts up to
10 MB. Use a source where the subject and important details are clearly visible.

Choosing an image prepares a preview in your browser. Continuing starts the upload. If the page
reports that editing is unavailable, you can prepare your instruction, read the guides, or check
availability again. Signing in does not by itself restore an unavailable service.

## 2. Write a clear prompt

Write a concrete instruction for the edit. Describe the change you want to see rather than a general
topic. You can revise the prompt before submitting the job. For example: "Replace the background
with a pale gray studio backdrop. Keep the mug, logo, and viewing angle unchanged."

This is a suggested prompt, not a demonstrated result. For more examples, read
[How to write AI image editing prompts](/blog/ai-image-editing-prompts).

## 3. Check access and output settings

Choose an available image product and output setting. When the guest trial is available, Nano Banana
2 Lite 1K offers one private, watermarked preview without registration. Other options require the
account access shown in the editor. Guest results expire within 24 hours and are not saved to History.

Model menus use matching brand icons across the navigation and editor. The configured catalog includes
twelve models across Nano Banana, GPT Image, and Seedream, including GPT Image 2.5 Flare,
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, and Seedream 4.0. Each menu lists the models currently enabled for use. A **Paid plan** badge marks
models that need a subscription; **Up to 2K** or **Up to 4K** describes a model's maximum resolution.
Choose the actual resolution in output settings. If your account needs a different plan, selecting
the model keeps you in the editor and shows an access notice. Click **View plans** to compare plans,
or **Keep editing** to close the dialog. Your selected model and prompt stay saved even if you have
not uploaded an image yet.

For an account generation, sign in, check the credit cost on **Generate image** or **Start edit**, and click
once. Safety checks and the credit reservation run automatically. If the price changes, the button shows
the updated cost and you can choose whether to submit again. Choosing a product or
entering a prompt does not itself purchase a plan. See [Image editing](/docs/image-editing) for output
settings and [Pricing](/pricing) for plan details.

## 4. Review the result

The result panel reports progress and the final outcome. A text generation shows a single image;
a reference edit offers a before-and-after comparison. Check text, faces, logos, and edges before
downloading or publishing. Original artwork on model pages is creative inspiration, not a verified
output or quality comparison of the named model. An eligible saved account
result can be used with **Edit again**, which starts a new edit with its own credit charge.

Use **Close preview** above a result to hide it while keeping your prompt and reference image.
Switching models or returning to the homepage also closes the preview. Your result stays in
**History** and the recent creations list, where you can open it again. A direct link to a selected
result still opens that preview, including after a refresh.

In the **History** panel, use **Back to history** above a result to return to the list and open
another creation. The top-right close button closes the whole panel.

## If an edit does not finish

If the upload fails, check the file format and size, then use the retry action. The page preserves the
selected file and prompt while you remain there. A pending, paused, or failed reference blocks
submission; retry or remove it explicitly to return to text generation. After submission, use its status and credit
details to check whether it is still processing or has failed. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same
edit while its outcome is uncertain.

An instruction that the safety check cannot approve asks you to revise and submit it again, or contact
support. That stopped request does not continue automatically. An unsupported language is identified
separately. Only a temporarily unavailable check suggests trying again later. These stops happen before
generation and do not charge generation credits.

“Preparing your result” includes private storage and image safety checks. Once an image is approved,
you can view and download it while finalization and credit settlement finish in the background.
The credit summary updates automatically; **Edit again** becomes available once the task completes.
You can also reopen the result from History without submitting another generation.

[Credits](/docs/credits) explains reservations and releases. [Contact support](/contact) if the status
remains unresolved; include the visible error message, but do not send private access links or passwords.
