## Two ways to start

**Have an idea?** Leave the reference image empty and describe the image you want. Sign in to
generate from text.

**Have a photo?** Upload a reference and describe the change. Mention the details you want to keep,
such as a person's features, a product label, or the camera angle. An available guest trial can
help you try reference editing before creating an account.

The editor shows the models, output settings, access requirements, and credit cost available to
you. Selecting a model, copying a prompt, or preparing an edit does not start generation.

## When something gets in the way

- **The image will not upload:** check the supported format and size beside the upload control.
  Retry the upload or remove it before continuing. [Working with references](/docs/image-editing#add-or-replace-a-reference).
- **Your edit is taking longer:** read its current status before starting another copy.
  [Progress and recovery](/docs/image-editing#follow-progress-without-starting-over).
- **Credits still look reserved:** a result may still be finishing or being checked.
  [Reserved, charged, and returned credits](/docs/credits#reserved-charged-and-returned).
- **Your draft came back without its image:** choose the source file again. A saved prompt does
  not always include the image file. [Resume after signing in or refreshing](/docs/quick-start#resume-after-signing-in-or-refreshing).

## Find a useful starting prompt

[Editing guides](/blog) explain how to write instructions and solve common editing problems.
[AI Effects](/effects) collects ready-to-use photo recipes as reviewed presets become available.
A preset prepares your prompt and settings; you still review the cost and choose when to generate.

Your own uploads and results do not automatically appear in these public collections.
Read [privacy and private media](/docs/privacy) before sharing an image or its access link.
