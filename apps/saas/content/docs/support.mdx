Sign in and open [Support](/settings/support) to create a ticket or continue a conversation.
Choose your personal account or an organization you currently belong to. Tickets
and replies stay within that account's access permissions.

Include a short subject, what happened, and an order or task reference when
available. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, API keys, or active
private media links. Support tickets do not accept file attachments.

## Follow a reply

Open the ticket in Support to read replies and send more information. A support
agent can change its status as they investigate. Organization access is checked
when you open or reply to a ticket; leaving an organization removes that access.

## Ask about a payment

Use the reference shown in Billing or your payment receipt. A refund request does
not mean the payment provider has completed the refund. Processing or uncertain
results need confirmation before another request is made.

Confirmed refunds recover the credits or access issued by that purchase. Credits
already spent can create an account debt, and a current subscription refund can
end the subscription under its billing policy. Any additional credit adjustment
is handled separately. Support will explain the result for your order.

For access problems before signing in, use [Contact](/contact). For rights,
impersonation, or other content concerns, use [Report content](/contact#report-content).
The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) describes support records and service providers.
