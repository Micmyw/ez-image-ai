The editor shows the credit cost before you start an image. It depends on the selected model and
output settings. Check that amount again after changing a model, resolution, or quality option.

Preparing a prompt, selecting a preset, uploading a reference, or browsing plans does not itself
start a paid generation. Current prices, plan allowances, and available payment methods are shown
on [Pricing](/pricing) and in **Credits & billing**.

The **3 images for $1.00** offer is a separate, one-time image pack. You can close its banner;
it stays hidden in the current browser tab for the rest of the session. The pack remains
available on the [image pack page](/image-pass).

## Before you generate

A registered **Free** account receives one **10-credit sign-in allowance** for the lifetime of
the account. It is enough for two Nano Banana 2 Lite 1K generations at 5 credits each.
There is no recurring monthly Free credit grant. Free accounts can use Nano Banana 2 Lite 1K;
other image models and HD or higher-quality options require an effective paid subscription,
including when using an image pass. Video and individual templates have their own eligibility rules.

Existing granted, purchased, and subscription credits keep their original balance and expiry;
the new allowance does not top up an account that already received a sign-in grant.
Review the current quote in the editor. New images generated with account credits have no guest watermark and a 30-day retention target;
download them before they expire.

Choose an available model and output option, then read the cost on **Generate image** or
**Start edit**. Your account must have the required access and enough available credits.

If the price changes before the request is accepted, the editor shows the new cost so you can
decide whether to submit again. A new generation, including **Edit again** or a retry after a
completed failure, has its own quote and credit charge.

When a guest trial is offered, its daily allowance is separate from an account balance.
Guest previews are watermarked and temporary. The editor shows the current trial availability;
an effect preset does not provide extra free edits.

The sign-in allowance does not expire. Signing in again, spending it, or starting another month
does not grant it again. Existing credit debt is repaid before the allowance increases your
available balance. Normal model access and content-safety rules still apply.

## Reserved, charged, and returned

| Credit status            | What it means                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reserved**             | Credits are held for an accepted generation while its outcome is being completed or checked. |
| **Charged**              | These credits were used for the completed attempt under the policy shown for it.             |
| **Released or returned** | This amount has been returned to the available balance after the attempt was settled.        |

Read the result panel for the amounts associated with that edit. A result can be visible while its
credit summary is still finishing. A reservation is not a completed refund.

A slow response or lost connection does not prove a generation failed. If the panel says
**Checking result**, wait for that existing attempt to be resolved instead of submitting another
copy. Confirmed temporary generation failures with no output return their reserved credits;
the panel shows the returned amount after settlement.

## If a safety check stops an image

Prompts or source images stopped before generation do not consume generation credits.

If the generation provider rejects sensitive input or output after submission, a new credit-funded
attempt uses the full quoted credits, even on the first rejection and even if no image is returned.
This rejection does not use the account's one-time output-review waiver. Technical failures and
uncertain outcomes keep their existing recovery and release rules. Older accepted attempts retain
the policy shown for their original quote.

For images returned and then blocked by our safety check, each billing account or team has one lifetime credit waiver for its first
confirmed content-safety rejection. Later blocked results are charged under the policy shown for
that attempt, even though the image cannot be displayed or downloaded. The result notice shows
whether the waiver applied and the actual amount charged or returned.

An incomplete check or technical failure is not the same as a confirmed content block.
Read the reason and credit outcome together. Revise the prompt or source before trying again,
or [contact support](/contact#report-content) if you believe a decision is mistaken.
See the [Terms of Service](/terms) for the content rules.

## Subscriptions and Credit Packs

**Subscriptions** provide credits for each monthly credit period. Annual billing changes when
you pay; subscription credits still arrive monthly and unused credits expire at the end of
their credit period. New annual subscriptions cost $114 for Pro, $294 for Ultimate, or $474
for Max per year, including renewals. That is 50% less than twelve monthly payments of $19,
$49, or $79. Pro, Ultimate, and Max still receive 700, 1,800, or 3,000 credits each month.
Existing subscriptions keep their current terms. Check your plan and dates in Credits & billing.

**Credit Packs** are one-time purchases that add credits without changing your subscription.
Review the amount, any applicable bonus, price, and expiry before paying. A pack is separate
from subscription renewal.

Canceling renewal preserves benefits for the period already paid for. The billing page shows
the cancellation status and paid-through date. A request still awaiting confirmation does not
mean recurring billing has already stopped.

An existing subscription or unfinished checkout may need to be resolved before another
subscription can begin. Use the options shown in Billing instead of starting repeated payments.

## Return from checkout safely

Returning to EzImageAI is not proof that a payment succeeded. Credits are added after the
payment is verified. Use **Resume checkout** or **Check status** when those actions are offered.

If you were charged but the order is unresolved, do not pay again. Contact support with the
displayed order reference and payment status. Never send your password, full card details, or
private image-access links.

Refunds adjust the credits and benefits from the corresponding purchase. If those credits were
already used, a balance debt can be repaid from future grants. The outcome depends on the
purchase and refund; check Billing or contact support for your order.

## Raindance video credits

The [Raindance guide](/blog/raindance-ai-trend) includes Solo and Duet video presets.
Images, ordinary videos, Hotel Lobby, Raindance and Rumpelstiltskin use the same valid account
credits, including gifted credits. Purchase discounts do not change where credits can be used.
Each order displays its complete quote before confirmation; content review, credit expiry,
refunds, debt and account access still apply. Reservations and returns follow the same account
ledger protections. The $1 image-pass offer remains a separate choice and cannot fund video.
The scene preparation and five-second silent video belong to the same order.
Free prompts and the guest image allowance do not include free video generation.
Reopening an accepted order or downloading it again does not create another generation charge.
