EzPic uses one Credit balance to meter image editing jobs. The current plan and server capability
response determine which image products and output settings you can submit. Each available combination
has its own Credit charge, which the review step shows before confirmation.

## Quality and resolution

Higher quality and resolution tiers within the same model use more Credits. Open output settings
to see the amount for each available tier; the selected amount updates before you continue.
Some models pair quality with resolution, so changing one also updates the other. Only the
combinations supported by the selected model are available.

For example, Seedream 5 Lite uses 7 Credits for 2K Basic, 10 for 3K High, and 14 for 4K Ultra.
GPT Image 2 uses 7, 11, or 17 Credits for 1K, 2K, or 4K. Choosing PNG, JPEG, or a supported
background option does not change the Credit amount. The guest trial remains sponsored.

## Reservation and settlement

Credits are reserved when a generation job is created. A successful job records the charged amount
and releases any unused reservation. A failed or canceled job reports the amount released according
to the server-side ledger result.

The job panel is the authoritative place to see the reservation, charge, and release values for that
edit. If the service cannot yet confirm the outcome, credits can remain reserved while that same
attempt is checked. An uncertain attempt is not automatically treated as failed or free.

## Results blocked by content safety

Each account or team gets one lifetime credit waiver for its first generated result blocked by a
confirmed content-safety rejection. Later blocked results are charged at the amount quoted before
generation, even though the image cannot be shown or downloaded. The result panel and job details
show whether the waiver was used and the actual credits charged and returned.

Instructions or uploaded images stopped before generation do not consume generation credits or the
waiver. Incomplete reviews and technical safety-check failures also do not consume the waiver; a job
with no usable output because of these failures releases its reservation. Sponsored guest trials
do not use the account waiver. Existing quotes retain their original billing policy.

Change the instruction or reference image before trying again after a content block. If you believe
the decision was mistaken, [contact support](/contact#report-content) with the job reference.

Safety notices separate the reason, credit outcome, and next step. When a reliable category is
available, it is explained in plain language. If the check only reports restricted content, the
notice says that a more specific reason is unavailable. An incomplete review is not a confirmed
policy violation. The notices link to the [content policy](/terms) and support; result notices
include the task reference to help support locate the edit. A sponsored guest trial also shows
whether the trial itself was consumed, separately from account credits.

Check [Pricing](/pricing) for current plan prices and allowances, and [Image editing](/docs/image-editing)
for individual output options. If a reservation stays unresolved, [contact support](/contact) with the
visible job status instead of submitting repeated copies of the edit.

## Subscription credits

Pro, Ultimate, and Max receive 700, 1,800, and 3,000 credits per monthly credit period. Annual
billing changes the payment cadence, not the grant cadence: credits still arrive monthly and unused
subscription credits expire at the end of their period. Any free account allowance is shown in the
account and is separate from the temporary guest trial. With current edits costing 5–25 EzPic Credits, those paid
monthly allowances cover approximately 28–140, 72–360, and 120–600 single-image edits respectively;
the actual count depends on the selected image products and output settings.

Canceling automatic renewal keeps your plan benefits until the end of the period already paid for.
For annual plans, the remaining prepaid monthly credit grants continue on schedule. Access ends when
the paid period ends; a past-due subscription retains access only during its stated grace period.
A full refund of the latest payment removes that access even during a grace period. Earlier
payments do not restore it.

Switching payment methods follows the same rule in both directions, PayPal to Waffo and Waffo to
PayPal. First cancel renewal in Billing, then wait for the payment service to confirm cancellation
and for the paid period to end. A cancellation request or a local expiry alone does not confirm
that recurring charges have stopped. Billing shows the original payment method, cancellation
progress and the paid-through date; unconfirmed requests are retried automatically.
If the next month was already paid before cancellation, that paid month remains available and you
can switch after its end date. One-time Credit Packs do not have this subscription restriction.

If you leave checkout before approval, the pricing page lets you resume the existing checkout and
check its status. Starting another subscription remains unavailable until the first checkout closes
or the existing subscription has confirmed cancellation and its paid period has ended.
Contact support if you need help with an unfinished checkout.

## Credit Packs

Credit Packs are one-time additions to your balance:

|          Pack | Price | Paid-subscriber amount |
| ------------: | ----: | ---------------------: |
| 1,500 credits |   $59 |          1,800 credits |
| 3,000 credits |  $109 |          3,600 credits |
| 5,000 credits |  $169 |          6,000 credits |
| 8,000 credits |  $259 |          9,600 credits |

Paid subscribers receive the displayed 20% bonus when the checkout is first created. That amount is
then fixed for the purchase even if the subscription changes later. Credit Pack credits expire six
calendar months after the verified purchase and do not change your subscription plan.

New subscription and Credit Pack checkout uses the currently available PayPal or Waffo options.
Payment choices refresh automatically; a temporarily unavailable method does not prevent using
another available method. EzPic adds credits only after the
payment has been verified; returning to the site is not itself proof of payment. Refunds reduce the
corresponding grant, and credits already used from a refunded purchase can create a balance debt that
future grants repay.

Partial refunds recover the corresponding proportion of the purchased credits, including any bonus.
For annual subscriptions, recovery counts both credits already issued and future monthly grants;
the earliest credit periods are adjusted first. Remaining paid plan benefits continue until the
original paid-through date unless the payment is fully refunded. A full refund or payment reversal
withdraws that payment's benefits and remaining credit grants.

A full refund of the latest PayPal or Waffo subscription payment also starts cancellation of
automatic renewal. Its plan benefits end immediately. The billing area shows whether cancellation
is still awaiting confirmation; unconfirmed attempts are retried automatically. Once the payment
provider confirms cancellation and the refunded credits are accounted for, you can subscribe again
immediately using any available plan or payment method, without waiting for the original end date.
Waffo may report cancellation in progress before confirming it; resubscription stays unavailable
during that time. Partial refunds and refunds of older payments do not cancel a newer paid period.
Canceling renewal without a full refund preserves the prepaid period and allows resubscription
after that period ends. This subscription limit does not apply to one-time Credit Packs.

If a refunded credit is reserved for an unfinished image, canceling or failing that image does not
make the refunded credit spendable again. If the image succeeds, the consumed refunded credits become
debt. New credits repay outstanding debt before becoming available for another image.
