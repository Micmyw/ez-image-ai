EzPic uses one Credit balance to meter image editing jobs. The current plan and server capability
response determine which image products and output settings you can submit. Each available combination
has its own Credit charge, which the review step shows before confirmation.

## Quality and resolution

Higher quality and resolution tiers within the same model use more Credits. Open output settings
to see the amount for each available tier; the selected amount updates before you continue.
Some models pair quality with resolution, so changing one also updates the other. Only the
combinations supported by the selected model are available.

For example, Seedream 5 Lite uses 7 Credits for 2K Basic, 10 for 3K High, and 14 for 4K Ultra.
GPT Image 2 uses 7, 11, or 17 Credits for 1K, 2K, or 4K. Choosing PNG, JPEG, or a supported
background option does not change the Credit amount. The guest trial remains sponsored.

## Reservation and settlement

Credits are reserved when a generation job is created. A successful job records the charged amount
and releases any unused reservation. A failed or canceled job reports the amount released according
to the server-side ledger result.

The job panel is the authoritative place to see the reservation, charge, and release values for that
edit. If the service cannot yet confirm the outcome, credits can remain reserved while that same
attempt is checked. An uncertain attempt is not automatically treated as failed or free.

Check [Pricing](/pricing) for current plan prices and allowances, and [Image editing](/docs/image-editing)
for individual output options. If a reservation stays unresolved, [contact support](/contact) with the
visible job status instead of submitting repeated copies of the edit.

## Subscription credits

Pro, Ultimate, and Max receive 700, 1,800, and 3,000 credits per monthly credit period. Annual
billing changes the payment cadence, not the grant cadence: credits still arrive monthly and unused
subscription credits expire at the end of their period. Any free account allowance is shown in the
account and is separate from the temporary guest trial. With current edits costing 5–25 EzPic Credits, those paid
monthly allowances cover approximately 28–140, 72–360, and 120–600 single-image edits respectively;
the actual count depends on the selected image products and output settings.

## Credit Packs

Credit Packs are one-time additions to your balance:

|          Pack | Price | Paid-subscriber amount |
| ------------: | ----: | ---------------------: |
| 1,500 credits |   $59 |          1,800 credits |
| 3,000 credits |  $109 |          3,600 credits |
| 5,000 credits |  $169 |          6,000 credits |
| 8,000 credits |  $259 |          9,600 credits |

Paid subscribers receive the displayed 20% bonus when the checkout is first created. That amount is
then fixed for the purchase even if the subscription changes later. Credit Pack credits expire six
calendar months after the verified purchase and do not change your subscription plan.

New subscription and Credit Pack checkout uses PayPal or Waffo. EzPic adds credits only after the
payment has been verified; returning to the site is not itself proof of payment. Refunds reduce the
corresponding grant, and credits already used from a refunded purchase can create a balance debt that
future grants repay.
